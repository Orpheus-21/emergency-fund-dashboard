Part of [[Emergency fund(log)]]. Follow these steps before I take cash out of the fund.

1. Write down the exact event in one sentence.
2. Apply the [[Emergency fund test]]. If one answer is no, stop. Do not take the cash.
3. Read "Max withdrawal now" in the Status table of [[Emergency fund(log)]].
4. If the cost is more than the maximum, pay the rest from the bank. If the event passes the test and the bank cannot pay, use rule 5 in [[Emergency fund rules]].
5. Take the cash.
6. Add the new total to the Log the same day. Write the reason in the Note column. See [[Emergency fund how to log]].
7. Read "Replenishment owed". This is the first target for my next surplus.
