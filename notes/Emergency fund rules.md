Part of [[Emergency fund(log)]]. This note holds the rules of my emergency fund.

The fund pays for small money needs. It does not pay for big emergencies. A big emergency empties the fund at once.

Only cash in my hands counts. Money in the bank does not count.

## Rules

1. The fund is cash in my hands. Bank money, investments, crypto, business money, and money for planned purchases do not count.
2. The fund is defensive money. It is not spending money.
3. The single-event limit and the floor are in [[Emergency fund settings]]. The maximum withdrawal is the smaller of two numbers: the limit, or the balance minus the floor. The dashboard shows this number as "Max withdrawal now".
4. The drawdown limit is a share of the peak balance. [[Emergency fund settings]] shows the share. When the dashboard shows a BREACH, replenishment is the first priority.
5. Below the floor, the maximum withdrawal is ₹0. One exception exists. A real emergency that passes the [[Emergency fund test]] can go over the limit. The Withdrawals table marks it OVER LIMIT. I must then replenish it first.
6. Every withdrawal creates a replenishment debt. The debt is the peak balance minus the balance now. The dashboard shows it as "Replenishment owed".
7. Surplus money goes in this order. First, the fund. Second, normal spending. Third, investments and projects.
8. A bigger fund does not give me permission to spend more. It only gives me a bigger buffer.
