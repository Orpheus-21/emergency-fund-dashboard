Part of [[Emergency fund(log)]]. This test decides if an expense can use the fund. See also [[Emergency fund rules]] and [[Emergency fund checklist]].

An expense qualifies only if all three answers are yes:

1. The expense is unexpected.
2. The expense is necessary.
3. To wait for it causes a real problem.

These do not qualify: games, keyboards, books, clothes, meals out, impulse purchases, planned purchases, upgrades, investments, trading losses, and "this would be useful".

If I only want to keep the bank balance high, the answer is no.
